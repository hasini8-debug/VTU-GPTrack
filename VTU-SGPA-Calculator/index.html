What is SGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) represents the academic performance of a student during a particular semester. It is calculated using the grade points obtained in each course and the credits assigned to those courses.

SGPA = Total Credit Points / Total Credits

How to Calculate SGPA Manually?

Step 01: Prepare Subject Details

Collect course credits, SEE marks and internal average marks for all subjects.

Step 02: Calculate Final Marks

Reduce SEE marks from 100 to 50 and add internal marks to obtain final marks.

Step 03: Find Grade Point

Convert final marks into the corresponding VTU grade point.

Step 04: Calculate Credit Points

Credit Point = Credits × Grade Point

Step 05: Calculate Total Credit Points

Add all subject credit points to obtain total credit points.

Step 06: Calculate Total Credits

Add credits of all registered subjects.

Step 07: Calculate SGPA

SGPA = Total Credit Points / Total Credits

NOTE :

⚠️ The manually calculated SGPA can be compared with the SGPA generated by this calculator for verification.

This calculator currently supports the VTU P24 Scheme only. Calculation methods and grading rules may vary depending on university regulations.
