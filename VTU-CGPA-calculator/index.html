What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) represents the overall academic performance of a student across multiple completed semesters. It is calculated using the SGPA and credits of each semester.

CGPA = Σ (SGPA × Semester Credits) / Σ Semester Credits

How to Calculate CGPA?

Step 01: Enter Semester Details

Enter the SGPA and total credits for each completed semester.

Step 02: Calculate Credit Points

Multiply the SGPA of each semester by its corresponding semester credits.

Step 03: Calculate Total Credit Points

Add the credit points obtained from all completed semesters.

Step 04: Calculate Total Credits

Add the total credits of all completed semesters.

Step 05: Calculate CGPA

Divide the total credit points by the total credits to obtain the final CGPA.
